🌿 HOW TO PLAY
⛏️ Digging
Tap DIG to find a random seed. The cooldown starts at 4 min and shrinks as you level up. Seeds also drop on their own every ~3 min, and you'll find a few when you come back after being away.
🌱 Planting
Tap a seed, then tap an empty pot. Or hit PLANT to fill every empty pot at once (it uses your selected seed, or your rarest seeds first).
✨ Golden Mutations
Every seed you plant has a 2% chance to turn GOLDEN (10% during 🌈 Rainbow weather). Golden plants grow 1.5× bigger, pay 3× coins and 2× XP, and drop an extra seed.
💧 Watering
Each watering cuts 20% off the grow time and adds a little size. Water refills 1 charge every 10 min (every 5 min when it's raining). Watering a wilted plant revives it.
🌦️ Weather
The weather changes every hour. 🌧️ Rain: water refills twice as fast and plants grow bigger. 🌬️ Windy: twice as many seed drops. 🌈 Rainbow: golden plants 5× more likely and +25% coins. 🌕 Blood Moon: Epic and Legendary seeds drop twice as often.
📜 Quests & 🔥 Streaks
You get 3 new daily quests at midnight. Clear all three for a bonus seed. Visit every day to build your streak: days 3 and 7 give free rare and epic seeds.
♻️ Compost
Turn 10 Common, 15 Uncommon, 20 Rare, 30 Epic or 40 Legendary seeds of one kind into one rarer seed. Composting Legendaries can give you a MYTHIC. The better the seeds you put in, the better the seed you get back.
🥀 Wilting
Once a plant is ready, it starts wilting after another full grow time (at least 2 hours). Wilted plants only give 65%. If you ignore one for 6× that long, it dies. Water a wilted plant to revive it.
📏 Plant Size
Every harvest rolls a random height. Rare oversized rolls go from 1 in 20 all the way up to 1 in 50,000, so even a lucky clover can beat a World Tree. The tallest plant each week wins the board.
⭐ Levels & Prestige
Levels unlock free pots, faster digging, more water and better seed odds. At Level 50 you can prestige: your level resets, but you earn a ✨ star and +10% coins from every harvest after that.
🌿 Strains & THC
Weed strains grow full plants with fan leaves and buds. Every bud harvest rolls a THC %: MIDS, GOOD, FIRE 🔥 (1.5× coins), TOP SHELF (2×) or EXOTIC 💎 (3×). Golden plants, fertilizer and the Terpene Lab all raise THC. At 4:20 (AM and PM, and all day on April 20th) strains drop 5× as often and buds get +3% THC.
🏆 Late Game
Spend your coins in the shop. Upgrades are permanent and stay through prestige (faster growth, bigger plants, more coins, better odds). Machines automate your garden: the Auto-Harvester, Auto-Planter, a Weather Machine and a Seed Vault. Crates (Bud Box, Bouquet, Mythic seeds and more) open the moment you buy them, and the seeds go straight into your bag. Style has pot skins and leaderboard titles. You can expand the greenhouse up to 40 pots. After LVL 50, XP keeps counting toward Mastery ranks, each worth +2% coins forever.
