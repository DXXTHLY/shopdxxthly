🌿 HOW TO PLAY
⛏️ Digging
Tap DIG to find random seeds. Cooldown starts at 4 min but shrinks as you level up. Seeds also drop passively every ~3 min!
🌱 Planting
Tap a seed in your bag to select it, then tap an empty plot to plant. Each plant has a grow timer — check the timer below the pot.
💧 Watering
Use water to speed up growth by 20% per watering. Water refills over time. Higher levels unlock more max water!
♻️ Compost
Compost 10 seeds of the same type to get a rare+ seed. Rare seeds need 10, Epic need 20, Legendary needs 40! Quality in = quality out.
📏 Plant Size
Every plant grows to a random height! Your biggest harvest of each species shows in the Codex. The tallest plant each season wins the leaderboard!
⭐ Levels & Prestige
Earn XP by digging, planting and harvesting. Higher levels unlock more plots, faster digging and more water. Hit Level 50 to Prestige — resets your level but earns a ✨ star next to your name on the board!
🌸 Seasons
Seasons change weekly. Some plants grow faster and drop more often in their season. The leaderboard resets each season — new champion every week!
🏆 Leaderboard
Set your name in the Board tab. You're scored on: biggest plant, total harvests, or total XP. Check back each season!
