The Backstory

About Me

I'm Brian, 33, from Maryland. I spent most of my 20s doing things I'm not proud of — drinking daily, running from anxiety, avoiding everything. July 3rd, 2023 was my last drink.

Sobriety didn't fix everything overnight. But it gave me the drive to actually build things instead of just thinking about them.

I have a neurological condition that makes my hands shake and anxiety that sometimes makes standing feel like a challenge. Regular jobs are hard. So instead of stressing about it, I started making stuff.

Music. A browser game. A 3D-printed hardware device that real people buy. All by myself, all from a sober house in Maryland, all because building something beats sitting around doing nothing.

Not looking for sympathy. Just building, shipping, and staying out of trouble.
